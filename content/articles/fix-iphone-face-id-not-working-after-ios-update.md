## Method 1: Force Restart Your iPhone

Software glitches are a common cause of Face ID malfunctions, including the 'Move iPhone Lower' loop or 'Face ID Is Not Available' error. A force restart can clear these temporary issues without affecting your data.

1.  **For iPhone 8 or later (including iPhone SE 2nd and 3rd generation):**
    a.  Quickly press and release the **Volume Up** button.
    b.  Quickly press and release the **Volume Down** button.
    c.  Press and hold the **Side** button until you see the Apple logo.
2.  **For iPhone 7 or iPhone 7 Plus:**
    a.  Press and hold both the **Volume Down** and **Side** buttons simultaneously.
    b.  Continue holding until you see the Apple logo.
3.  **For iPhone 6s or earlier (including iPhone SE 1st generation):**
    a.  Press and hold both the **Home** and **Side** (or Top) buttons simultaneously.
    b.  Continue holding until you see the Apple logo.

After your iPhone restarts, attempt to use Face ID.

## Method 2: Inspect TrueDepth Camera and Face ID Settings

The TrueDepth camera system, which powers Face ID, requires an unobstructed view. Incorrect settings can also prevent Face ID from functioning.

1.  **Clean the TrueDepth Camera:** Gently wipe the notch area (where the front camera and sensors are located) with a soft, lint-free cloth. Ensure no dirt, smudges, or debris are covering the sensors. Remove any bulky cases or screen protectors that might be obstructing the area.
2.  **Verify Face ID Settings:**
    a.  Open **Settings**.
    b.  Tap **Face ID & Passcode**.
    c.  Enter your device passcode when prompted.
    d.  Ensure that **Face ID** is enabled for the features you intend to use (e.g., **iPhone Unlock**, **iTunes & App Store**, **Wallet & Apple Pay**, **Password Autofill**).
    e.  Check if **Require Attention for Face ID** is enabled. If you wear glasses or have specific facial features, try toggling this off temporarily to see if it resolves the issue, then re-enable for security.

## Method 3: Reset Face ID and Re-enroll

Corrupted Face ID data can lead to persistent errors. Resetting Face ID clears the existing biometric data, allowing you to set it up fresh.

1.  Open **Settings**.
2.  Tap **Face ID & Passcode**.
3.  Enter your device passcode.
4.  Tap **Reset Face ID**.
5.  Once Face ID is reset, tap **Set Up Face ID**.
6.  Follow the on-screen instructions to re-enroll your face. Ensure your face is centered, well-lit, and that you complete both scans as directed.

## Method 4: Update iOS or Reset All Settings

Outdated iOS versions can contain bugs that affect Face ID functionality. If a software bug is deeply embedded or if conflicting settings are causing the problem, an update or a settings reset may be necessary.

1.  **Update iOS:**
    a.  Open **Settings**.
    b.  Tap **General**.
    c.  Tap **Software Update**.
    d.  If an update is available, tap **Download and Install** and follow the prompts. Ensure your iPhone is connected to Wi-Fi and has sufficient battery or is connected to power.
2.  **Reset All Settings (Last Resort before Hardware Diagnosis):** This action resets all system settings to their defaults but does not erase your data, apps, or media. It can resolve underlying configuration conflicts.
    a.  Open **Settings**.
    b.  Tap **General**.
    c.  Tap **Transfer or Reset iPhone**.
    d.  Tap **Reset**.
    e.  Tap **Reset All Settings**.
    f.  Enter your passcode if prompted and confirm the action.

If Face ID continues to fail after these steps, it may indicate a hardware issue with the TrueDepth camera system. In such cases, contact Apple Support or visit an authorized service provider for further diagnosis and potential repair.
